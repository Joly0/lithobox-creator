Lithobox Creator: lithophane light box and lamp generator

Lithobox Creator turns your photos into lithophanes you can 3D print: thin reliefs that show the picture when light shines through. Design a light box, lamp or ornament in the browser and download it as STL or 3MF for Bambu Studio, OrcaSlicer or any slicer. It is free, open source, and your photos never leave your device.

Shapes: box with up to five pictures, flat picture frame or light box, cylinder lamp, arc, polygon lamp, lampshade, ball lamp, ornament (circle, star, hexagon, heart) and heart.

Features: crop and tone each photo, lamp preview, colour lithophanes (cyan, magenta and yellow layers), lid closures, lamp sockets, battery trays, project files and share links. Available in English, German, French, Spanish and Italian.
